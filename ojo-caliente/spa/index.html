Before you arrive

Visit guidelines.

  • Reservations are required; book at least four weeks ahead.
  • Arrive 30 minutes early to change into a robe; a pre-treatment soak loosens the muscles.
  • Bring sandals and a refillable water bottle. The spa is at altitude, so hydrate and rest.
  • Share health concerns when booking: blood pressure, heart conditions, pregnancy, allergies or injury.
  • Cancel or reschedule 24+ hours ahead at no charge; later changes are charged in full.
  • Prices exclude tax and gratuity. Spa services are for guests 18+.
  • A refundable day soaking pass is required for spa services at Ojo Caliente.

Age policy · 13+

  • Ojo Spa Resorts are designed for guests 13 and older.
  • Children under 13 may stay overnight and dine, but may not enter soaking, pool or spa areas.
  • Guests under 18 must be accompanied by an adult at all times. Spa treatments are for guests 18+.
