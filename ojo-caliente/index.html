Restoration update: the Arsenic Pool has reopened. A new cold plunge and pool-deck restoration are under construction.

Ojo Caliente is one of the only hot springs on Earth with four healing minerals (Arsenic, Lithia, Soda and Iron) in naturally sulfur-free waters. More than 100,000 gallons rise from volcanic aquifers every day.

Soak in twelve communal hot springs or reserve a private pool with its own kiva fireplace, then dine on produce grown a short walk from the kitchen at the Artesian Restaurant.
