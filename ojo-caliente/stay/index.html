Included with every stay

Before you choose a room, know this.

  • Unlimited soaking, 7:30 AM – 10 PM, incl. check-in (before 3 PM) & check-out (after 11 AM) days
  • Complimentary 75-minute morning yoga, 9 AM or 10:45 AM
  • Suite & home guests: extended Kiva Pool hours, 6 AM – midnight
  • Posi & Cliffside: a private pool or tub on your patio
  • Free WiFi · hiking & biking trails
  • Two robes in every room · Ojo botanical teas
  • No resort fees

Age policy · 13+

  • Ojo Spa Resorts are designed for guests 13 and older.
  • Children under 13 may stay overnight and dine, but may not enter soaking, pool or spa areas.
  • Guests under 18 must be accompanied by an adult at all times. Spa treatments are for guests 18+.
