The ritual of the waters · Ojo Caliente

Four sulfur-free springs. One sacred source.

Iron, Soda, Lithia and Arsenic rise from the earth beneath the cliffs, as they have for centuries. Choose a mineral, find your temperature, and learn what each spring has long been believed to restore.

Iron Pool

It’s believed that soaking in iron-rich springs can aid your immune system.

Inhale piñon smoke and fresh sage. Our kitchens cook with what the season gives, and the land around the springs is yours to wander: trails, gardens, a quiet labyrinth and hammocks strung between the cottonwoods.
