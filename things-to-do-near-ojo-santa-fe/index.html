What to Do Near Ojo Santa Fe: A Guide to La Cienega Valley and Beyond

Seventy-seven high-desert acres, twenty minutes from the Plaza, and a valley worth slowing down for.

Ojo Santa Fe sits in the La Cienega Valley, south of the city, on the road to Rancho de las Golondrinas.

On property

Walk more than five miles of private trails, visit the puppies, join morning yoga or swing in a hammock by the pond.

In town

Canyon Road, the Railyard and the Baca District hold hundreds of galleries; summer brings the Spanish Colonial, Indian and Folk Art Markets.

Getting around

The resort shuttle runs to Santa Fe Airport and the NM-599 Rail Runner station for $30 each way. For everything else, rent a car.
