The Ojo Farm · Ojo Caliente

Not farm to table.Seed to plate.

A regenerative 1.5-acre farm a thousand feet from the Artesian kitchen door, feeding both resorts.

Our farmer and chefs choose seeds together, time the harvest together and taste every dish before it reaches the table.

The farm pairs regenerative agriculture with modern technology, reading New Mexico’s microclimates hill by hill and valley by valley. It grows fruit, vegetables, botanicals and flowers; creates agricultural jobs for local New Mexicans; and sells enough produce to support community nutrition and the local economy.

What grows

Harvests change every week.

From May through November the farm supplies salad greens for the Ojo Farm Salad, poblanos for the Poblano Fries and sides like broccolini, calabacitas and mashed potatoes.
