Ojo destinations

Beyondthe springs.

Two of America’s great art towns sit within an easy drive of our resorts: Santa Fe and Taos.

Home of Ojo Santa Fe

Santa Fe

The “City Different”, at 7,200 feet at the base of the Sangre de Cristo Mountains.

Galleries
Hundreds of galleries across historic Canyon Road, the revitalised Railyard and the Baca District.
Summer markets
The internationally acclaimed Spanish Colonial, Indian and Folk Art Markets.
Palace of the Governors
Native American-made jewellery and crafts, a tradition generations deep.
Red or green?
New Mexico cuisine built on chile. Ask for “Christmas” and get both.

Taos

The “Soul of the Southwest”, one of America’s leading art colonies since the 1920s.

Taos Pueblo
A UNESCO World Heritage Site.
Taos Ski Valley
World-class skiing in the Sangre de Cristos.
The Enchanted Circle
A scenic loop through mountains, rivers and gorges.
Rio Grande rafting
Whitewater on a National Wild & Scenic River.
