About Ojo Spa Resorts

Honoringsacred springs.

Travellers and locals have been drawn to these springs for thousands of years. We are here to steward them, and to share them.

Two resorts in Northern New Mexico’s high desert, both above 6,000 feet, both first settled by Indigenous Puebloans, both built around natural spring bathing.

Ojo Spa Resorts is a family-owned group. Our properties pair therapeutic soaking with award-winning spa treatments, farm-to-table dining from the largest resort-owned and operated farm in the U.S., and the trails, art and culture of the land around them.

Ojo Santa Fe Spa Resort

Ojo Caliente Mineral Springs

Established in 1868 and one of the oldest natural health resorts in the country. Its water rises from a subterranean volcanic aquifer, sulfur-free and rich in arsenic, lithia, iron and soda.
