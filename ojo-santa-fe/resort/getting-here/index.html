From downtown Santa Fe (NM-599)

  1. 1From the NM-599 entrance at Hwy 285 / St. Francis Drive, head south on NM-599 for 12.5 miles.
  2. 2Turn right at the light onto the I-25 frontage road.
  3. 3Turn right onto Los Pinos Road, following signs for Rancho de las Golondrinas.
  4. 4Continue 2.6 miles; the resort is on your left.
  5. 5At the gated Guest Entrance, use the call box on the left.

From Albuquerque (I-25)

  1. 1Take I-25 North about 45 miles to Exit 276 for NM-599.
  2. 2Keep right for NM-599 N / Los Alamos / Española, then move to the left-turn lane at the light.
  3. 3Turn left onto the I-25 frontage road, then right onto Los Pinos Road.
  4. 4Continue 2.6 miles; the resort is on your left.
  5. 5At the gated Guest Entrance, use the call box on the left.

Transportation

Arriving by air & rail.

  • Resort shuttle to Santa Fe Airport and the NM-599 Rail Runner station: $30 per person each way, 9 AM – 7 PM, reserve 48 hours ahead at 877-977-8212.
  • Private car: Santa Fe Valet 505-424-2171 · NM Black Car Service 505-316-1100.
  • Taxis and rideshare are often hard to find here; we strongly recommend a rental car.
